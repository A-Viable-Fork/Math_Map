## D7-001 Functor Application

*Transformer; construction; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Applies a functor F: C to D to an object or morphism of C. The basic structure-transporting move.

- input: Category C, functor F: C to D, object or morphism of C
- output: Object or morphism of D
- preserved: Composition and identities; commutative diagrams map to commutative diagrams
- broken: Injectivity on objects and morphisms (non-faithful or non-full functors lose distinctions)
- complexity: N/A

## D7-002 Natural Transformation

*Transformer; surgery; origin: fill; tier: authored*

A family of morphisms eta_X: F(X) to G(X), one per object, commuting with every F(f) and G(f). A morphism between functors.

- input: Parallel functors F, G: C to D
- output: Family eta = (eta_X) with naturality squares
- preserved: Naturality: uniform across all objects and morphisms
- broken: Nothing at the component level; the identity of the two functors
- complexity: N/A

## D7-003 Left Adjoint Construction

*Constructor; construction; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Builds F left adjoint to a given G: Hom(F X, Y) = Hom(X, G Y) naturally. The free, best-approximation-from-below construction.

- input: Functor G: D to C satisfying the solution-set condition
- output: Functor F: C to D with unit X to G F X
- preserved: Colimits (left adjoints preserve all colimits)
- broken: Limits in general; the extra structure is freely added, not determined by X
- complexity: N/A

## D7-004 Right Adjoint Construction

*Constructor; construction; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Builds G right adjoint to a given F: Hom(F X, Y) = Hom(X, G Y). The cofree, best-approximation-from-above construction.

- input: Functor F: C to D satisfying the dual solution-set condition
- output: Functor G: D to C with counit F G Y to Y
- preserved: Limits (right adjoints preserve all limits)
- broken: Colimits in general
- complexity: N/A

## D7-005 Left Kan Extension

*Transformer; construction, expansion; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Extends F: C to E along K: C to D to Lan_K F: D to E, universal among functors with a transformation from F. Pointwise a colimit over the comma category K/d.

- input: Functors F: C to E, K: C to D; E cocomplete enough
- output: Functor Lan_K F: D to E with unit F to (Lan_K F) K
- preserved: Universal property; colimits of F when K is to the terminal category
- broken: Values off the image of K are forced (freely generated), not given
- complexity: N/A

## D7-006 Right Kan Extension

*Transformer; construction, expansion; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Extends F: C to E along K: C to D to Ran_K F, universal among functors with a transformation to F. Pointwise a limit over d/K.

- input: Functors F: C to E, K: C to D; E complete enough
- output: Functor Ran_K F: D to E with counit (Ran_K F) K to F
- preserved: Universal property; limits of F when K is to the terminal category
- broken: Values off the image of K are forced (cofreely), not given
- complexity: N/A

## D7-007 Yoneda Embedding

*Transformer; embedding; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Sends each object c to its representable presheaf Hom(-, c). Fully faithful: an object is determined up to isomorphism by the morphisms into it.

- input: Locally small category C
- output: Presheaf category [C^op, Set] with embedding y
- preserved: Full faithfulness; limits of C; all morphism data
- broken: Colimits of C (y freely adds new ones); smallness of the ambient category
- complexity: N/A

## D7-008 Co-Yoneda Lemma Application

*Transformer; construction; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Writes a presheaf as a canonical colimit of representables (the density formula, or the coend form of F = integral^c Hom(-, c) x F c).

- input: Presheaf or functor F: C^op to Set
- output: Canonical colimit presentation of F over its category of elements
- preserved: F up to natural isomorphism
- broken: Economy: the presentation uses every element, not a minimal set
- complexity: N/A

## D7-009 Grothendieck Construction

*Constructor; construction; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Flattens an indexed category (a functor into Cat) into one total category fibered over the base. Objects are pairs (c, x in F c).

- input: Pseudofunctor F: C^op to Cat (or C to Cat)
- output: Fibration p: integral F to C (Cartesian or opCartesian)
- preserved: All data of F, recoverable as the fibers; equivalence of 2-categories between indexed categories and fibrations
- broken: The separation into levels (it becomes one category plus a projection)
- complexity: N/A

## D7-010 Limit Computation

*Transformer; construction; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Computes the universal cone over a diagram: the object that best maps into every part compatibly.

- input: Diagram D: J to C
- output: Limit object lim D with projections
- preserved: Every cone factors uniquely; right adjoints preserve it
- broken: Information not shared by all parts of the diagram
- complexity: N/A

## D7-011 Colimit Computation

*Transformer; construction; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Computes the universal cocone under a diagram: the object that best receives every part, glued along the diagram's morphisms.

- input: Diagram D: J to C
- output: Colimit object colim D with injections
- preserved: Every cocone factors uniquely; left adjoints preserve it

## D7-012 Pullback Construction

*Constructor; construction; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Forms the limit of X to Z from Y: the object of pairs agreeing in Z. Fiber products, intersections, inverse images.

- input: Cospan X to Z from Y
- output: Object X x_Z Y with projections
- preserved: Universal property; monomorphisms and many classes stable under pullback
- broken: Parts of X and Y not over a common point of Z
- complexity: N/A

## D7-013 Pushout Construction

*Constructor; construction; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Forms the colimit of a span X from Z to Y: X and Y glued along Z. Amalgamations, attaching cells, unions along an overlap.

- input: Span X from Z to Y
- output: Object X +_Z Y with injections
- preserved: Universal property; epimorphisms stable under pushout
- broken: Distinctness of the images of Z
- complexity: N/A

## D7-014 Equalizer Construction

## D7-016 Product Formation

## D7-018 Cartesian Closed Structure Evaluation

*Transformer; construction; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Uses the product and exponential of a Cartesian closed category: currying Hom(A x B, C) = Hom(A, C^B) and evaluation C^B x B to C.

- input: Cartesian closed category, objects A, B, C
- output: Curried or evaluated morphisms
- preserved: The currying bijection, natural in all variables; models of simply typed lambda calculus

Builds C^B, the internal object of morphisms from B to C, right adjoint to - x B.

- input: Objects B, C in a category with finite products
- output: Object C^B with evaluation map

## D7-020 Subobject Classifier Evaluation

*Transformer; construction; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Classifies a monomorphism m: S to X by its unique characteristic map chi: X to Omega pulling back true: 1 to Omega.

- input: Topos (or category with Omega), monomorphism m: S to X
- output: Characteristic map chi_m: X to Omega
- preserved: Sub(X) = Hom(X, Omega), naturally; internal logic (Heyting algebra structure)

## D7-021 Cauchy Completion

*Transformer; completion; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Adds to a (possibly enriched) category all absolute colimits: splittings of idempotents (and finite biproducts in the additive case). For metric spaces as enriched categories, the classical Cauchy completion.

- input: Category C, possibly V-enriched
- output: Cauchy complete category C-bar with C to C-bar fully faithful
- preserved: Presheaf category: [C^op, V] = [C-bar^op, V]; Morita equivalence class
- broken: Nothing already present; adds objects only
- complexity: N/A

## D7-022 Karoubi Envelope Construction

*Transformer; completion; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Forms Kar(C): objects are idempotents (X, e), morphisms f with f = e' f e. Splits every idempotent.

- input: Category C
- output: Karoubi envelope Kar(C) with full embedding C to Kar(C)
- preserved: Presheaves on C; Morita class; full faithfulness of the embedding
- broken: Nothing; freely adds retracts
- complexity: N/A

*Transformer; completion; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Closes a category under splitting of idempotents. The same construction as the Karoubi envelope, named by what it adds.

- input: Category C

- preserved: Presheaf category; Morita equivalence

- complexity: N/A

## D7-024 Freyd's Adjoint Functor Construction

*Constructor; construction; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Constructs a left adjoint to a continuous G from a small solution set, using limits in the domain (Freyd's General Adjoint Functor Theorem).

- input: Continuous functor G: D to C, D complete and locally small, with solution sets
- output: Left adjoint F: C to D
- preserved: G's limit preservation; the adjunction bijection

## D7-025 MacNeille Completion

*Transformer; completion; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Embeds a poset in the smallest complete lattice containing it, via cuts (A, B) with A = lower bounds of B and B = upper bounds of A.

- input: Poset P
- output: Complete lattice DM(P) with order embedding

## D7-027 Isbell Duality Transformation

*Transformer; duality; origin: fill; tier: authored*

The adjunction between presheaves and copresheaves on C, O(X)(c) = Nat(X, C(c, -)) and Spec(Y)(c) = Nat(C(-, c), Y). Generalizes algebra-geometry duality.

- input: Presheaf or copresheaf on small category C

- preserved: Representables are fixed; restricts to an equivalence on the Isbell-reflexive objects
- broken: Everything outside the reflexive fixed points (non-reflexive objects change)
- complexity: N/A

## D7-028 Opposite Category Dualization

## D7-031 Monad Resolution

*Transformer; decomposition; origin: fill; tier: authored*

Factors a monad T as G F for an adjunction F left of G. The resolutions form a category with Kleisli initial and Eilenberg-Moore terminal.

- input: Monad T on C

- preserved: The monad T
- broken: Uniqueness: many adjunctions resolve the same monad
- complexity: N/A

## D7-X01 Aufhebung of a level

*Correspondence; closure, expansion; origin: added; tier: receipted*

For a level i of a topos (an essential subtopos, with its adjoint modality, skeleton left of sheaf), the least level j above i at which every i-skeleton is a j-sheaf (Lawvere). In the colloquial form: the least k such that n-skeletal implies k-coskeletal. It need not exist.

- input: Topos with its lattice of levels; a level i
- output: The least level j resolving the opposite of i (every i-skeleton a j-sheaf), when it exists
- preserved: The lower level's objects: i-skeleta survive as j-sheaves
- broken: The opposition at level i: the skeleton side is absorbed into the higher level's sheaf side. The step depends on the shape category: n to n+1 for reflexive globular sets, n to 2n-1 for simplicial sets (n > 1), n to 2n for cubical sets
- complexity: N/A
- acts on: level to level
- named invariants: preserved skeletal-objects; broken level-opposition
- receipt (excerpts/nlab-aufhebung.txt): "iff it is a minimal level which resolves the opposites of level $i$"
- receipt (excerpts/nlab-aufhebung.txt): "amounts to saying that every $i$-skeleton is a $j$-sheaf"
- receipt (excerpts/arxiv-1003.5944.txt): "is called the Aufhebung of B′"
- receipt (excerpts/arxiv-1003.5944.txt): "for reﬂexive globular sets this is indeed the case"
- receipt (excerpts/arxiv-1003.5944.txt): "If a simplicial set is n-skeletal with n > 1, it is (2n− 1)-coskeletal."
- receipt (excerpts/arxiv-1003.5944.txt): "The Aufhebung relation for the topos of cubical sets is 2n."
