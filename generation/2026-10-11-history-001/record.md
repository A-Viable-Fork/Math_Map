# Generation: history as a constrained deformation (HISTORY-001)

## Reaching for

DEFORM-001 left a pattern that sounded like a theory of scientific change: deformation of the old symmetry algebra lists the possible next theories, and the conflict that forced the change picks one. Told that way it is easy to believe and easy to fake, because hindsight writes the constraint after it knows the answer. What I want to know is how much of the selection the conflict does, how much the predecessor does, and how much the person writing the constraint does. So the round is built around controls: the predictions committed before computing, a constraint that may only speak the predecessor's language, the same constraint on the wrong predecessor, every other constraint of the same shape as a decoy, and a generator cut back to the mathematics that existed before the transition.

## Seeds

- DEFORM-001's calibration (`generation/2026-10-11-deform-001/`): the four Galilei directions and the claim that Maxwell's invariance selects the relativistic one. Taken as the hypothesis, never as evidence.
- D7-X07, D7-X08, D7-X10, D7-X11 (receipted in DEFORM-001) and D7-X12 (central extension, receipted in this round): the generator's parts. Applied to history they break in one place that matters: the cohomology (1929, 1948) and deformation theory (1964) postdate most transitions, so a generator that uses them cannot have been the historical mechanism.
- Gandy's reformulation criterion (A-Viable-Fork/Physics `docs/research/GANDY-0_1.md`): a reformulation keeps the facts; a requirement any theory meets by reformulation is trivial. Taken as the reading of the reformulation rows: they should be invisible to H^2.
- The Poisson bracket (D4-301, 1809): a guess, before computing, that the central primitives of quantum mechanics and of Galilean mass were already in the classical vocabulary.

## Candidates

1. **The pattern holds on the deformation rows with few survivors**: for Galilei to Poincare, Poincare to de Sitter, the plane to Heisenberg and Galilei to Bargmann, the predecessor's candidate set filtered by the conflict leaves the successor at rank 1 with at most one alternative.
2. **The conflict does the selecting, not the predecessor**: the same constraint on a wrong predecessor reaches the successor too wherever the wrong predecessor is another contraction of it (Carroll for Poincare).
3. **The decoy count is the honest measure**: how often a random constraint of the same shape selects the successor says whether the real constraint carries information or whether the shape alone does the work.
4. **The time slice**: the successors' new brackets are reachable with Jacobi alone (Lie, 1870s to 1890s) and the central terms with the Poisson bracket, so the transitions were generable from their own vocabulary; what postdates them is only the organization of the candidates into a finite list.
5. **The reach**: unification, geometrization, reduction and the Lagrangian to Hamiltonian reformulation fall outside a finite algebra, and their share is the method's reach.

## Against the obvious

1. The obvious version is DEFORM-001's sentence, checked on one row with no control. This one is the same claim with its failure modes made countable.
2. The obvious reading takes the selection as evidence for the predecessor's role. This candidate expects the opposite.
3. This is not the obvious control. A permutation test would shuffle constraints across rows; the decoys here keep the row and vary the constraint within its form, so a hit means the form is too permissive, not that the rows are similar.
4. The obvious version slices by when a tool became standard. This one slices by first publication, which favors the generator, so a failure under it is robust.
5. This is the obvious version: an honest share.
