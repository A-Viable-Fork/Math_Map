# What the math map should keep: ranked design survey

Date: 2026-11-13. Kind: utility judgments proposed to the operator, based on read sources and bounded checks. No schema, source, mapfill record or production checker is changed.

The strongest near-term direction is a sparse layer of **claim-scoped obligations and useful equations**, connected by **restricted relations and explicitly lossy representation arrows**. The atlas then adds compatible views over these identified claims and operations. A cluster name, an executable example and a proof citation have different authority; the data must preserve that difference.

The full inventory has 64 data families. The generated catalog is deliberately broader than the immediate recommendation. A row earns a production place when a named reader can perform a useful function, its assumptions and receipts are explicit, and its unchecked remainder can be bounded. The operator rules on landing.

## The information architecture under consideration

| Layer | Records it could own | Boundary to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| Mathematical claim/operation | Identity, nature, typed operands, atomic propositions, predicates, quantities, laws, mathematical standing, counterexamples and obstructions | A map receipt is evidence about a specific claim, not the mathematical standing of every statement about its object |
| Representation and realization | Formula and sentence spans; proof/code/platform/model references; contract versions; conventions; kept guarantees and residuals; scoped checks | The runtime/model/foundation and the direction of each arrow determine what transports |
| Use and negative knowledge | Typed problem classes, recipes, inverse/adjoint/specialization edges, failed routes, reopening conditions and observed performance | A failed attempt is bounded; an impossibility claim needs an obstruction theorem |
| Atlas views | Charts, shared identity on overlaps, valid transition domains, coherence witnesses, local admission and dependency invalidation | Isomorphism, quotient, refinement and analogy have different contracts; compatibility is checked only at the declared equality level |

Inferred from the atlas reading: its charts are consistent relational views of one library. Its paper does not define an executable transition-map format or a cocycle checker. Those are generated designs here. The finite quadratic and fixed-point trials show what such records would need, while the graphic-matroid witness shows why transporting an isomorphism label indiscriminately fails.

## Ranked verdicts

The ordinal rank is a design priority judgment, not a numerical truth/confidence score. Confirmed means **confirmed worth keeping for the stated function**, not permission to land every pilot row. Plausible has a useful path with evidence or integration still owed. Needs refinement names the missing semantics or checker. Marginal stays peripheral until a consumer demonstrates value. Dead means the proposed function should be declined.

Grades: 12 confirmed, 30 plausible, 17 needs refinement, 4 marginal, 1 dead. The first six are the six selected experimental priorities; their landing readiness differs after testing. Full arrows, day-one reader changes, receipt rules and evidence-debt costs for every rank are in verdicts.json and the frozen generation record.

## First landing proposal: scoped source-applicability obligations (C02)

This is a concrete proposal for a later implementation round. The first deliverable is a small optional sidecar and a reader. It keeps the current map fields and their evidence rules intact. An obligation says when a particular cited justification may be used. A false guard rules out that justification in that context; it is not a theorem that the mathematical operation is impossible by every other route.

**Proposed file:** mapfill/claim-obligations.js, with a documented data-only export CLAIM_OBLIGATIONS. Begin with four individually reviewed claim scopes: the quotient-cone contractibility claim (D4-075), the sheafification adjunction (D3-116), the explicit leading-coefficient degree-product law (D7-X14), and roots-membership for a nonzero polynomial (D7-X20). The sixteen trial guards are a queue, not a blanket landing batch.

| Field | Meaning and required check |
|---|---|
| id | Stable obligation id, e.g. D7-X20:roots-membership:nonzero; unique and never reused for a different claim |
| owner | Entry id plus exact claim target: field, quoted span and field hash, or a named declaration/claim id; stale target text invalidates the row |
| appliesTo | The cited justification and version, not every possible use of the whole entry |
| binders | Named operands, carrier/type and where the caller binds them; no undeclared free variables |
| requires | Small typed AST: boolean or integer facts with eq/ne/lt/gt initially; explicit conjunction can follow only with defined truth/unknown rules |
| factScope | Operand identity, theorem/model/foundation and fact provenance; a supplied assumption remains labeled as an assumption |
| ambient | Other hypotheses of the cited theorem, each recorded or explicitly unresolved; a selected guard never claims to be the complete premise list |
| receipts | Exact source pin, source hash, quoted context/statement offsets and a reviewed mapping from each guard to the theorem premise |
| evidence | Grade of this guard claim only; imported formal proof evidence requires the existing statement/definition/axiom discipline |
| counterexamples | Optional targeted witness, failed guard, attacked claim id and executable or formal receipt |

Example target: Polynomial.mem_roots at Mathlib 380f2aa, on D7-X20. Its explicit hp : p != 0 becomes the guard polynomial.nonzero = true, attached only to the roots-membership equivalence. The entry input supplies a proposed commutative-domain context; the landing review must fetch and receipt the actual inherited class/section context as well. The existing short theorem-line excerpt alone does not establish that complete context.

**Checker change:** mathmap.mjs validates ids, claim targets, typed ASTs, declared variables, receipt occurrence and explicit ambient obligations. compose.mjs gains an optional fact environment keyed by operand/claim id and emits obligation results: satisfied, violated, unknown or stale. Missing facts, ill-typed facts, unsupported predicates and missing ambient premises fail closed. No obligations recorded means applicability is unknown, not vacuously established. The invariant evidence is never raised just because a caller supplied a true boolean.

**Evidence rule:** every claimed premise needs its full source statement and inherited context, checked under the strict reading rule. Quoting a name or a partial conjunct does not confirm a predicate. Facts supplied by a model are assumptions unless independently supported; a finite example receipts only that example. An external proof is admitted only after local statement fidelity and axiom checks. Counterexamples target the cited claim and domain.

**First reader:** compose.mjs, consumed through the existing MCP compose_chain tool. Its response exposes unresolved obligations along with present kind/invariant results. Research_OS mapread.mjs then needs an explicit projection of declared typing and obligation ids; today it drops both the relevant conditions and relation metadata. A field-use manifest/conformance fixture (C44) should show that this reader actually consumes the new records.

**Acceptance demonstration:** preserve all existing map outputs when the sidecar is unused; refuse a supplied -2 curve for the smooth inverse-contraction justification; refuse the zero-divisor degree equality justification; keep an absent Nonempty/HasWeakSheafify fact unknown; distinguish a correct typed fact from an unsupported assertion; invalidate a target after its text changes. Gate with node scripts/check-all.mjs and keep statement review separate from those executable checks. No new schema or evidence promotion is authorized by this proposal.

## Recommended order after that first proposal

1. Land the small C02 reader and the four reviewed obligations, with minimal C01 claim targets and C26 negative cases. Check scope and source context before enlarging the row count.
2. Add narrow C23 rules on the transforms/polynomial APIs already consumed, carrying C10 conventions, C11 operator-law kinds and C13 output-domain obligations. Expose a real MCP rule query.
3. Specify C40 contract hashing precisely, then pilot C30 realization variants separately: existing public platform rows, code, externally admitted proofs (C31/C49), and only then model or empirical artifacts. Private rows remain in their source repositories by pin.
4. Add C15 inverse routes with C03 named operands and reviewed image domains. Resolve C19 identity only where two consumers need the same canonical operation; do not merge homonyms automatically.
5. Add C33 translation cards as proposals with explicit losses, then C34 certificates on a genuinely restricted fragment. A semantically checked language round trip is a separate milestone from a JSON card round trip.
6. Build one local atlas region using C51 charts and C52 overlaps. Admit C53 transitions only with witnesses, test C54 generating cycles at a named equality level, retain C55 analogies/losses, and add C47/C57 invalidation only for the overlaps actually used.

Keep C62 consumer coverage and evidence debt beside every round. Keep source identity, mathematical standing, formal assumptions, platform assurance, observed readings and utility judgments separate. The operator can choose each landing batch from this survey without accepting a universal schema redesign.
