# Math map

A typed map of mathematical transformations across nine domains: each entry gives an operation's input, output, preserved and broken invariants, with tags from a controlled vocabulary, an origin, a trust tier and an evidence level per claim (README.md). D9, systems and effects, carries the operation contracts the research OS composes, with Android and NixOS bindings.

@.infra/CONVENTIONS.md

## Checks

- `node scripts/check-all.mjs` before every commit: the parser and vocabulary checks (`mathmap.mjs --check`), the audit, the Mathlib and Lean links, freshness of every generated file (`build.mjs --check`), the composition and MCP self-tests, the blind and catalogue checks, the kit's checks (`.infra/run.mjs`), and freshness of the five reports. Commit only on PASS, and gate the commit on the command's exit status.
- After a change to `mapfill/`, `source/` or `audit/`: `node scripts/build.mjs`, then regenerate the reports (`node scripts/mathmap.mjs`, `audit.mjs`, `topology.mjs`, `type-lexicon.mjs`, `gaps.mjs`).
- After adding or removing a file: describe it (a `Role:` header in code, or a line in `repo-map-manifest.json`) and run `node .infra/tools/repo-map.mjs`.

## Layout

- `source/`: the landed map, verbatim. Never edited.
- `mapfill/`: everything this repository adds to it, each file with its own contract: fills, flags, additions, conditions, corrections, the invariant vocabulary, Mathlib links, Lean claims, generated D9 entries and their rounds, the Android and NixOS bindings (`mapfill/README.md`).
- `excerpts/`: verbatim windows receipting additions and corrections, each with its source and hash; `catalogue/` lists every paper cited and how deeply it was read.
- `generation/`: one directory per generation round (`record.md`, the generators' reports and packages, `grading.md`); `prompts/` the prompts that started them.
- `audit/`, `lean/`, `scripts/`, `reports/`; `docs/inbound/` for dated notes from other repositories.
- Generated by `node scripts/build.mjs`, never edited by hand: `README.md`, `map.json`, `domains/*.md`, `audit.json`, `MANIFEST.json`, `SHA256SUMS`. Change the build, not its output.

## Rules

- Every entry keeps its origin (map, fill, clone, added, generated) and its tier; a stronger tier is earned by evidence, never assigned.
- An addition or a correction carries receipts: verbatim windows made with `node scripts/excerpt.mjs` (`--wiki` pins the page revision), quoted at the claim. A correction needs a prior finding (an audit grade, a flag or a Mathlib conflict) and keeps the landed text beside it.
- A flag withholds a wrong entry from use; it never rewrites it.
- Generated entries land only from a graded round, at their own tier, with the round recorded in `mapfill/generated.js`; a round whose text this repository corrected says so and names the generator's ids.
- Bindings are generated claims with hashed sources, never a device or host test; a hash is reproduced here before it is marked so.
- Mathlib is pinned at `380f2aa` (`mapfill/formal.js`); links and Lean claims are made at that pin.
- A held finding for an outside project (an upstream bug, a patch) is recorded and held until the operator signs off.
