'''Whitehead's lemmas''' (named after [[J. H. C. Whitehead]])

Let <math>\mathfrak{g}</math> be a finite-dimensional, semisimple Lie [[algebra over a field]] of characteristic zero, ''V'' a finite-dimensional [[Lie_algebra_representation#Formal_definition|module]] over it

The first Whitehead lemma is an important step toward the proof of [[Weyl's theorem on complete reducibility]].
