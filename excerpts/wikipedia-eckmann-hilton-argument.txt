the '''Eckmann–Hilton argument''' (or '''Eckmann–Hilton principle''' or '''Eckmann–Hilton theorem''') is an [[argument]] about two [[unital magma]] structures

is an [[argument]] about two [[unital magma]] structures on a [[Set (mathematics)|set]] where one is a [[homomorphism]] for the other.

The above proof also has a "two-dimensional" presentation that better illustrates the application to higher [[homotopy group]]s.

Given this, the structures are the same, and the resulting [[magma (algebra) |magma]] is a [[commutative monoid]].

Then <math>\circ</math> and <math>\otimes</math> are the same and in fact commutative and associative.

This establishes that the two operations coincide and are commutative.
