a '''clone''' is a [[Set (mathematics)|set]] ''C'' of [[finitary]] [[operation (mathematics)|operation]]s on a set ''A'' such that

*''C'' contains all the [[projection (set theory)|projection]]s

*''C'' is closed under [[generalized composition|(finitary multiple) composition]] (or "superposition")

the set of operations on its carrier definable by a ''σ''-[[first-order logic#Formation rules|term]] (the ''term functions'') is a clone.
