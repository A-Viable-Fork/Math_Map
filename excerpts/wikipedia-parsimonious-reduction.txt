a '''parsimonious reduction''' is a transformation from one problem to another (a [[Reduction (complexity)|reduction]]) that preserves the number of solutions.

Polynomial-time parsimonious reductions are a special case of a more general class of reductions for counting problems, the [[polynomial-time counting reduction]]s.
