{{Short description|Ideal of the nilpotent elements}}

The nilradical of a commutative ring is the intersection of all [[prime ideal]]s.

The nilradical of a commutative ring is the set of all [[nilpotent element]]s

If the nilradical is the zero ideal, the ring is called a [[reduced ring]].
