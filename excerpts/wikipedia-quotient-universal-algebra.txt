we can define the [[Closure operator|closure]] of any [[binary relation]] ''E'', with respect to a fixed algebra <math>\mathcal{A}</math>, such that it is a congruence

<math> \langle E \rangle_{\mathcal{A}} = \bigcap \{ F \in \mathrm{Con}(\mathcal{A}) \mid E \subseteq F \}</math>

Note that the closure of a binary relation is a congruence

the compatibility conditions are used to give the classes an algebraic structure.

a '''quotient algebra''' is the result of [[Partition of a set|partition]]ing the elements of an [[algebraic structure]] using a [[congruence relation]].

The quotient algebra has these classes as its elements, and the compatibility conditions are used to give the classes an algebraic structure.

Quotient algebras are also called '''factor algebras'''.
