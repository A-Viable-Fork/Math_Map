The '''Daubechies wavelets''', based on the work of [[Ingrid Daubechies]], are a family of [[orthogonal wavelet]]s defining a [[discrete wavelet transform]]

characterized by a maximal number of vanishing [[Moment (mathematics)|moments]] for some given [[Support (mathematics)|support]].

D4 encodes polynomials with two coefficients, i.e. constant and linear signal components
