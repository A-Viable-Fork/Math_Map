'''Tucker decomposition''' decomposes a [[tensor]] into a set of matrices and one small core tensor.

Taking <math>d_i = n_i</math> for all <math>i</math> is always sufficient to represent <math>T</math> exactly, but often <math>T</math> can be compressed or efficiently approximately by choosing <math>d_i < n_i</math>.
