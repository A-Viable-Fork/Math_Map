the '''Todd class''' is a construction in the [[algebraic topology]] of [[characteristic class]]es.

The Todd class of a [[vector bundle]] can be defined in terms of [[Chern class]]es within various theories

:<math>\operatorname{td}(E) = \prod Q(\alpha_i),</math>

which is to be computed in the [[cohomology ring]] of <math>X</math>

The Todd class is multiplicative:

::<math>\operatorname{td}(E\oplus F) = \operatorname{td}(E)\cdot \operatorname{td}(F).</math>
