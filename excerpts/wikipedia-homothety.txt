a '''homothety''' (or '''homothecy''', or '''homogeneous dilation''') is a [[Transformation (mathematics)|transformation]] of an [[affine space]] determined by a point {{mvar|S}} called its ''center'' and a nonzero number {{mvar|k}} called its ''ratio''

<math display="block">\mathbf x'=\mathbf s + k(\mathbf x -\mathbf s).</math>

* A ''line'' is mapped onto a parallel line. Hence: ''angles'' remain unchanged.

* The ''ratio of two line segments'' is preserved.

Both properties show that a homothety is a ''[[Similarity (geometry)|similarity]]''.

In Euclidean geometry, a homothety of ratio <math>k</math> multiplies ''distances'' between points by {{tmath|\vert k \vert}}, ''areas'' by <math>k^2</math>
