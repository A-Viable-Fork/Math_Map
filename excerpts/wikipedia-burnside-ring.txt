Given a [[finite group]] ''G'', the generators of its Burnside ring Ω(''G'') are the formal sums of isomorphism classes of finite [[Group action (mathematics)|''G''-sets]]. For the [[Ring (mathematics)|ring structure]], addition is given by [[disjoint union]] of ''G''-sets and multiplication by their [[Cartesian product]].

The Burnside ring is a free '''Z'''-[[Module (mathematics)|module]], whose generators are the (isomorphism classes of) [[Group action (mathematics)|orbit types]] of ''G''.
