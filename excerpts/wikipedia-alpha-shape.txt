Alpha shapes are closely related to alpha complexes, which are subcomplexes of the [[Delaunay triangulation]] of the point set.

Each edge or triangle of the Delaunay triangulation may be associated with a characteristic radius: the radius of the smallest empty circle containing the edge or triangle.

For each [[real number]] <math>\alpha</math>, the  <math>\alpha</math>-complex of the given set of points is the [[simplicial complex]] formed by the set of edges and triangles whose radii are at most <math>1/\alpha</math>.

More specifically, {{harvtxt|Edelsbrunner|1995}} showed that the two shapes are [[homotopy equivalent]].
