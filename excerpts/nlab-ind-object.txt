An **ind-object** of a [[category]] $\mathcal{C}$ is a **formal [[filtered colimit]]** of objects of $\mathcal{C}$.

Here &quot;formal&quot; means that the colimit is taken in the [[category of presheaves]] of $\mathcal{C}$

1. The embedding $\mathcal{C}\to ind$-$\mathcal{C}$ should be [[full and faithful functor|full and faithful]],

1. the objects of $\mathcal{C}$ should be [[compact object|compact]] in $ind$-$\mathcal{C}$.
