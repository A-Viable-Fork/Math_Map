In general, a square complex matrix ''A'' is [[similar (linear algebra)|similar]] to a [[block diagonal matrix]]

:<math>P^{-1}AP = J;</math> that is, <math>AP = PJ.</math>

Then a basis with respect to which the matrix representing the operator has the required form exists [[if and only if]] all [[eigenvalue]]s of the operator lie in ''K''

and it is the number of Jordan blocks corresponding to ''λ''<sub>''i''</sub>.

* The sum of the sizes of all Jordan blocks corresponding to an eigenvalue ''λ''<sub>''i''</sub> is its [[algebraic multiplicity]].
