== The Künneth spectral sequence ==

:<math>E_{pq}^2 = \bigoplus_{q_1 + q_2 = q} \mathrm{Tor}^R_p(H_{q_1}(X; R), H_{q_2}(Y; R)) \Rightarrow H_{p+q}(X \times Y; R).</math>

the homology of ''X'' and ''Y'' is related to the homology of their product by a  Künneth [[spectral sequence]]

In the cases described above, this spectral sequence collapses to give an isomorphism or a short exact sequence.
