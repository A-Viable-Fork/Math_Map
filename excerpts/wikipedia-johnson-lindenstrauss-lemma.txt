concerning low-distortion [[embedding]]s of points from high-dimensional into low-dimensional [[Euclidean space]].

Given <math>0 < \varepsilon < 1</math>, a set <math>X</math> of <math>N</math> points in <math>\mathbb{R}^n</math> , and an integer <math>k > 8(\ln N)/\varepsilon^2</math>,

: <math>(1-\varepsilon)\|u-v\|^2 \leq \|f(u) - f(v)\|^2 \leq (1+\varepsilon)\|u-v\|^2</math>

there exists a linear function <math>f: \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^k</math>

To obtain the projection algorithmically, it suffices [[with high probability]] to repeatedly sample orthogonal projection matrices at random.
