'''Serre''' and '''localizing subcategories''' form important classes of [[Subcategory|subcategories]] of an [[abelian category]].

Let <math>\mathcal{A}</math> be an [[abelian category]]. A non-empty full [[subcategory]] <math>\mathcal{C}</math> is called a ''Serre subcategory'' (or also a ''dense subcategory''), if for every short [[exact sequence]]

In words: <math>\mathcal{C}</math> is closed under subobjects, quotient objects

Each Serre subcategory <math>\mathcal{C}</math> of <math>\mathcal{A}</math> is itself an abelian category, and the inclusion functor <math>\mathcal{C}\to\mathcal{A}</math> is [[Exact functor|exact]].
