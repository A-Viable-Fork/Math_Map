the '''dual graph''' of a [[planar graph]] {{mvar|G}} is a graph that has a [[vertex (graph theory)|vertex]] for each [[face (graph theory)|face]] of {{mvar|G}}

For instance, [[cycle (graph theory)|cycles]] are dual to [[cut (graph theory)|cuts]], [[spanning tree]]s are dual to the [[complement set|complements]] of spanning trees

The definition of the dual depends on the choice of embedding of the graph {{mvar|G}}
