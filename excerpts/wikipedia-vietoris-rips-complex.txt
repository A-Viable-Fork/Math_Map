the '''Vietoris–Rips complex''', also called the '''Vietoris complex''' or '''Rips complex''', is a way of forming a [[topological space]] from [[finite set]]s of points and the [[distance]]s between them.

The collection of all Vietoris–Rips complexes is a commonly applied construction in [[persistent homology]] and [[topological data analysis]], and is known as the [[Vietoris–Rips filtration|Rips filtration]].
