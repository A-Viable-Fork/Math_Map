It can also be defined as a curve whose points are at a constant ''[[normal distance]]'' from a given curve.

*As for [[parallel (geometry)|parallel lines]], a normal line to a curve is also normal to its parallels.

*<math>\vec x'_d(t) \parallel  \vec x'(t),\quad</math> that means: the tangent vectors for a fixed parameter are parallel.

*<math>k_d(t)=\frac{k(t)}{1+dk(t)},\quad</math> with <math>k(t)</math> the  [[curvature]] of the given curve

*When parallel curves are constructed they will have [[Cusp (singularity)|cusp]]s when the distance from the curve matches the radius of [[curvature]].
