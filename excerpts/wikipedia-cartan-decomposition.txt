the '''Cartan decomposition''' is a decomposition of a [[Semisimple Lie algebra|semisimple]] [[Lie group]] or [[Lie algebra]]

It generalizes the [[polar decomposition]] or [[singular value decomposition]] of matrices.

Let <math>G</math> be a non-compact semisimple Lie group

* The subgroup <math>K</math> is a maximal compact subgroup of <math>G</math>, whenever the center of G is finite.

the maximal Abelian subalgebras <math>\mathfrak{a}</math> in <math>\mathfrak{p}</math> are unique up to conjugation by <math>K</math>.

:<math>G = KP = KAK,</math>
