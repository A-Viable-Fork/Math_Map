the '''Cremona group''', named after [[Luigi Cremona]], is [[Birational geometry#Birational automorphism groups|the group of birational automorphisms]] of the <math>n</math>-dimensional [[projective space]]

The Cremona group is naturally identified with the [[automorphism group]] <math>\mathrm{Aut}_k(k(x_1, ..., x_n)) </math> of the [[rational function|field of the rational function]]s

The [[projective general linear group]] <math>\mathrm{PGL}_{n+1}</math> is contained in <math>Cr_n</math>. The two are equal only when <math>n=0</math> or <math>n=1</math>
