* A square matrix <math>P</math> is called an '''orthogonal projection matrix''' if <math>P^2 = P = P^{\mathrm T}</math> for a [[real number|real]] [[matrix (mathematics)|matrix]], and respectively <math>P^2 = P = P^{*}</math> for a [[complex number|complex]] matrix

A projection is orthogonal if and only if it is [[self-adjoint operator|self-adjoint]].

It leaves its [[image (mathematics)|image]] unchanged.

Suppose the [[Linear subspace|subspace]]s <math>U</math> and <math>V</math> are the [[Image (mathematics)|image]] and [[Kernel (linear algebra)|kernel]] of <math>P</math> respectively.
