'''flips'''  and  '''flops''' are  codimension-2 [[surgery theory|surgery]] operations arising in the [[minimal model program]]

each of which contracts some curves on which the canonical divisor <math>K_{X_i}</math> is negative.

the flip of <math>X_i</math> is a birational map (in fact an isomorphism in [[codimension]] 1) <math>f\colon X_i \rightarrow X_i^+</math> to a variety whose singularities are 'better' than those of <math>X_i</math>.

is called the '''flip''' of <math>f</math> if <math>-K</math>  is relatively ample

In applications, <math>f</math> is often  a [[small contraction]] of an extremal ray

*<math>X</math> and <math>X^+</math> only have mild singularities, such as [[terminal singularities]].
