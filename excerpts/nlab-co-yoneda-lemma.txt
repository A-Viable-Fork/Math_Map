What is sometimes called the _co-Yoneda lemma_ or _density formula_ is a basic fact about [[presheaves]]

it says that every [[presheaf]] is a [[colimit]] of [[representable functor|representables]]

Under [[duality|abstract duality]] an [[end]] turns into a [[coend]], so a co-Yoneda lemma ought to be a similarly fundamental expression for $F(c)$ in terms of a [[coend]].

## Every presheaf is a colimit of representables

Yet another way to state this is as a [[colimit]] over the [[comma category]] $(Y,F)$, for $Y$ the [[Yoneda embedding]] (the [[category of elements]] of $F$):

is canonically presented as a colimit of representables, we exhibit a natural isomorphism

it is the &quot;colimit over itself of all the representables contained in it&quot;
