an '''isometry''' (or '''congruence''', or '''congruent transformation''') is a [[distance]]-preserving [[Transformation (mathematics)|transformation]] between [[metric space]]s

Let  <math>X</math> and <math>Y</math> be [[metric space]]s with metrics

:<math>d_X(a,b)=d_Y\!\left(f(a),f(b)\right).</math>

* Any [[reflection (mathematics)|reflection]], [[translation (geometry)|translation]] and [[rotation]] is a global isometry on [[Euclidean space]]s.
