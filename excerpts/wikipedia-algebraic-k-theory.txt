Quillen was led to define the ''K''-theory of ''R'' as the homotopy groups of ''BGL''(''R'')<sup>+</sup>.

This modification was called the [[plus construction]].

This new definition began with an [[exact category]]

The functor ''K''<sub>0</sub> takes a ring ''A'' to the [[Grothendieck group]] of the set of isomorphism classes of its [[finitely generated module|finitely generated]] [[projective module]]s
