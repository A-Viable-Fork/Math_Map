every module has '''free resolutions''',  '''projective resolutions''' and '''flat resolutions'''

which are left resolutions consisting, respectively of [[free module]]s, [[projective module]]s or [[flat module]

Projective resolution of a module <math>M</math> is unique up to a [[chain homotopy]]

Similarly every module has '''injective resolutions''', which are right resolutions consisting of [[injective module]]s.

Dually, every <math>R</math>-module possesses an injective resolution.

Projective resolutions (and, more generally, flat resolutions) can be used to compute [[Tor functor]]s.

is an [[exact sequence]] of [[module (mathematics)|module]]s

dually a '''coresolution''' or '''right resolution'''

a ''free resolution'' of a module <math>M</math> is a left resolution in which all the modules <math>E_i</math> are [[free module|free]] <math>R</math>-modules.

Every <math>R</math>-module possesses a free left resolution.

Similarly, resolutions that are acyclic for all the functors '''Hom'''(&nbsp;⋅&nbsp;, ''M'') are the projective resolutions and those that are acyclic for the functors '''Hom'''(''M'', &nbsp;⋅&nbsp;) are the injective resolutions.

Any injective (projective) resolution is ''F''-acyclic for any left exact (right exact, respectively) functor.

can be obtained from as the homology of ''F''-acyclic resolutions

Every flat resolution is acyclic with respect to this functor.

Resolutions are used to define [[homological dimension (disambiguation)|homological dimension]]s.

Similarly, flat dimension is used to define [[weak global dimension]].
