The [[internal hom]] $[S,X]$ in a cartesian closed category is often called [[exponential object|exponentiation]] and is denoted $X^S$.

There is an evaluation map $ev_{X, Y}: Y^X \times X \to Y$, which by definition is the map corresponding to the identity map $Y^X \to Y^X$ under the bijection.
