'''Čech complex''' is an [[abstract simplicial complex]] constructed from a point cloud in any [[metric space]]

In other words, the Čech complex is the [[Nerve of a covering|nerve]] of the set of ''ε''-balls centered at points of ''X''.

the nerve theorem provides a guarantee that the Čech complex is homotopy equivalent to union of the balls in the complex.
