a '''traced monoidal category''' is a category with some extra structure which gives a reasonable notion of feedback.

:<math>\mathrm{Tr}^U_{X,Y}:\mathbf{C}(X\otimes U,Y\otimes U)\to\mathbf{C}(X,Y)</math>

* dinaturality in <math>U</math>: for every <math>f:X\otimes U\to Y\otimes U'</math> and <math>g:U'\to U</math>
