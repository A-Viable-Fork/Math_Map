the '''nine lemma''' (or 3×3 lemma) is a statement about [[commutative diagram]]s and [[exact sequence]]s

* If all columns as well as the top and bottom rows are exact, and <math>A_2 \to C_2</math> is the zero morphism, then the middle row is exact.

* If all columns as well as the two bottom rows are exact, then the top row is exact.

is a statement about [[commutative diagram]]s and [[exact sequence]]s valid in the category of [[group (mathematics)|group]]s and any [[abelian category]].

* If all columns as well as the two top rows are exact, then the bottom row is exact.
