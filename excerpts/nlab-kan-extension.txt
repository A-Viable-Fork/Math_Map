there is an expression of a weighted (co)limit and hence a pointwise Kan extension as an ordinary (&quot;conical&quot;, meaning: in terms of [[cone]]s) (co)limit over a [[comma category]]:

If the [[codomain]] category $D$ admits certain [[limit|(co)limits]], then left and right Kan extensions can be constructed

the universal transformation $F\to (Lan_p F)p$

* the left Kan extension operation forms the [[colimit]] of a functor;

(Lan_p F)(c&#39;) \coloneqq colim^{C&#39;(p(-),c&#39;)} F

Then these objects fit together into a functor $Ran_p F$ which is a right Kan extension of $F$ along $p$.

* the right Kan extension operation forms the [[limit]] of a functor.

(Ran_p F)(c&#39;) \coloneqq lim^{C&#39;(c&#39;,p(-))} F
