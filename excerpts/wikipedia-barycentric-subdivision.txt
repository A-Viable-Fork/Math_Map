the '''barycentric subdivision''' is a standard way to subdivide a given [[simplex]] into smaller ones. Its extension to [[Simplicial complex|simplicial complexes]] is a canonical method to refining them.

The barycentric subdivision is an operation on simplicial complexes.

meaning that one replaces larger simplices with a union of smaller simplices.

Moreover, the induced map is an isomorphism: Subdivision does not change the homology of the complex.
