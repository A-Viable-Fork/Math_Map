It was introduced by [[Alexander Grothendieck]]

and developed by [[Pierre Berthelot]].

The idea of crystalline cohomology, roughly, is to replace the [[Zariski topology|Zariski open sets]] of a scheme by infinitesimal thickenings of Zariski open sets with  [[divided power structure]]s.

Its values <math>H^n(X/W)</math> are [[module (mathematics)|modules]] over the [[ring (mathematics)|ring]] <math>W</math> of [[Witt vector]]s over <math>k</math>.
