the '''radical''' of an [[ideal (ring theory)|ideal]] <math>I</math> of a [[commutative ring]] is another ideal defined by the property that an element <math>x</math> is in the radical [[if and only if]] some power of <math>x</math> is in <math>I</math>.

:<math>\sqrt{I} = \left\{r\in R \mid r^n\in I\ \hbox{for some}\ n \in \Z^{+}\!\right\},</math>

Equivalently, <math>\sqrt{I}</math> is the [[preimage]] of the ideal of [[nilpotent]] elements (the [[nilradical of a ring|nilradical]] of the ring) of the [[quotient ring]] <math>R/I</math>

A '''radical ideal''' (or '''semiprime ideal''' or '''reduced ideal''') is an ideal that is equal to its radical.
