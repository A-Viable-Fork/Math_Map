a '''Block LU decomposition''' is a [[matrix decomposition]] of a [[block matrix]] into a lower block triangular matrix ''L'' and an upper block triangular matrix ''U''. This decomposition is used in [[numerical analysis]] to reduce the complexity of the block matrix formula.
