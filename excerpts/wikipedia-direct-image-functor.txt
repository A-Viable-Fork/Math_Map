called the '''direct image sheaf''' or the '''pushforward sheaf''' of ''F'' along ''f'', such that the global sections of ''f''<sub>∗</sub>''F'' is given by the global sections of ''F''.

given a sheaf ''F'' defined on a [[topological space]] ''X'' and a [[continuous map]] ''f'': ''X'' → ''Y''

:<math>f_*F(U) := F(f^{-1}(U)).</math>

They are called '''higher direct images''' and denoted ''R<sup>q</sup> f''<sub>∗</sub>.

one likewise has the right derived functor

:<math>Rf_*: D_{qcoh}(X) \to D_{qcoh}(Y)</math>
