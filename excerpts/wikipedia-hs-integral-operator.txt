a '''Hilbert–Schmidt integral operator''' is a type of [[integral transform]].

:<math>(Tf) (x) = \int_{\Omega} k(x, y) f(y) \, dy</math>

:<math>\Vert T \Vert_\mathrm{HS} = \Vert k \Vert_{L^2}.</math>

Hilbert–Schmidt integral operators are both [[continuous function|continuous]] and [[compact operator|compact]].

:<math>\int_{\Omega} \int_{\Omega} | k(x, y) |^{2} \,dx \, dy < \infty ,</math>
