the '''Hilbert symbol''' or '''norm-residue symbol''' is a function

Over a local field <math>K</math> with [[multiplicative group]] of non-zero elements <math>K^\times</math>,

the quadratic Hilbert symbol is the [[function (mathematics)|function]] <math>K^\times\times K^\times\to\{\pm 1\}</math> defined by

:<math>(a,b)=\begin{cases}+1,&\mbox{ if }z^2=ax^2+by^2\mbox{ has a non-zero solution }(x,y,z)\in K^3;\\-1,&\mbox{ otherwise.}\end{cases}</math>

Equivalently, <math>(a, b) = 1</math> if and only if <math>b</math> is equal to the [[Field norm|norm]] of an element of the quadratic extension <math>K[\sqrt{a}]</math>.

The (bi)multiplicativity, i.e.,

:<math>(a,b_1b_2)=(a,b_1)\cdot(a,b_2)</math>
