The notion of *cyclic operads* ([Getzler &amp; Kapranov 1995](#GetzlerKapranov1995))

the [[cyclic group]] $\mathbb{Z}_{/(n+1)}$ which rotates the $n+1$ in/outputs into each other.

for a cyclic operad this is extended to an [[group action|action]] of $Sym(n+1)$
