'''random projection''' is a technique used to [[dimensionality reduction|reduce the dimensionality]] of a set of points which lie in [[Euclidean space]].

In random projection, the original <math>d </math>-dimensional data is projected to a <math>k </math>-dimensional subspace, by multiplying on the left by a random matrix <math>R \in \R^{k \times d} </math>.

The random matrix R can be generated using a Gaussian distribution.

then they may be projected into a suitable lower-dimensional space in a way which approximately preserves pairwise distances between the points with high probability.
