'''Fubini–Study metric''' (IPA: /fubini-ʃtuːdi/) is a [[Kähler metric]] on a [[complex projective space]] '''CP'''<sup>''n''</sup> endowed with a [[Hermitian form]].

A Fubini–Study metric is determined up to [[homothety]] (overall scaling) by invariance under such a U(''n''&nbsp;+&nbsp;1) action; thus it is [[homogeneous space|homogeneous]].

A consequence of this formula is that the sectional curvature satisfies <math>1 \leq K(\sigma) \leq 4</math> for all 2-planes <math>\sigma</math>.
