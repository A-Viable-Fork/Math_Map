* A local ring ''R'' with [[maximal ideal]] ''m'' is called '''Henselian''' if  Hensel's lemma holds.

* A Henselian local ring is called '''strictly Henselian''' if its [[residue field]] is [[separable extension|separably closed]].

For any local ring ''A'' there is a universal Henselian ring ''B'' generated by ''A'', called the '''Henselization''' of ''A''

such that any [[local homomorphism]] from ''A'' to a Henselian ring can be extended uniquely to ''B''.

Similarly there is a strictly Henselian ring generated by ''A'', called the '''strict Henselization''' of ''A''.

More precisely it depends on the choice of a separable algebraic closure of the residue field of ''A''
