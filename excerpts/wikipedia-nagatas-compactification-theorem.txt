'''Nagata's compactification theorem'''

implies that every [[abstract variety]] can be embedded in a [[complete variety]]

shows that a [[Glossary of scheme theory#separated|separated]] and [[Finite morphism#Morphisms of finite type|finite type morphism]] to a [[Noetherian scheme]] ''S'' can be factored into an [[Glossary of scheme theory#immersion|open immersion]] followed by a [[proper morphism]].
