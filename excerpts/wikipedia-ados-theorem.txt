'''Ado's theorem''' is a theorem characterizing finite-dimensional [[Lie algebra]]s.

The theorem was proved in 1935 by [[Igor Dmitrievich Ado]]

the theorem states that ''L'' has a [[linear representation]] ρ over ''K'', on a [[finite-dimensional vector space]] ''V'', that is a [[faithful representation]]

every finite-dimensional [[Lie algebra]] ''L'' over a [[field (mathematics)|field]] ''K'' of [[characteristic zero]]

that is a [[faithful representation]], making ''L'' isomorphic to a subalgebra of the [[endomorphism]]s of ''V''.

can be viewed as a Lie algebra of [[square matrices]] under the [[commutator bracket]]
