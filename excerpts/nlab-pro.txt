A PRO, an abbreviation for &quot;product category&quot;, is similar to a [[PROP]] but more general

A **PRO** is a [[strict monoidal category]] $T$ in which every [[object]] is of the form

(\mathbb{N}, +, 0)

being merely a [[monoidal category]] but not necessarily [[symmetric monoidal category|symmetric monoidal]]
