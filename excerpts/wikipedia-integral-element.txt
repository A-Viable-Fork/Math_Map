The set of elements of <math>B</math> that are integral over <math>A</math> is called the '''integral closure''' of <math>A</math> in <math>B.</math>

if ''b'' is a [[root of a polynomial|root]] of some [[monic polynomial]] over ''A''.

an element ''b'' of a [[commutative ring]] ''B'' is said to be '''integral over''' a [[subring]] ''A'' of ''B''

The integral closure of any subring <math>A</math> in <math>B</math> is, itself, a subring of <math>B</math> and contains <math>A.</math>

In particular, the [[Krull dimension]]s of ''A'' and ''B'' are the same.

It is the first step in [[resolution of singularities]] since it gives a process for resolving singularities of [[codimension]] 1.
