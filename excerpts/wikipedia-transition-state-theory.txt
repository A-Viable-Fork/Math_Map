By modeling reactions as Langevin motion along a one dimensional reaction coordinate, [[Hans Kramers|Hendrik Kramers]] was able to derive a relationship between the shape of the potential-energy surface along the reaction coordinate and the transition rates of the system.

In the overdamped (or "diffusive") regime, the transition rate from state ''A'' to ''B'' is related to the resonant frequency of the wells via

k^{A\rightarrow B} = \frac{\omega_a\omega_H}{2\pi\gamma}\exp\left(-\frac{E_H-E_A}{k_\text{B}T}\right)
