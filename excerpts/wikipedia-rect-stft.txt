a '''rectangular mask short-time Fourier transform''' (rec-STFT) is a simplified form of the [[short-time Fourier transform]]

In rec-STFT, a '''rectangular window''' (a simple on/off time-limiting function) is used to isolate short time segments of the signal.

: <math>X(t,f)=\int_{t-B}^{t+B} x(\tau) e^{-j2\pi f\tau} \, d\tau</math>

The [[Rectangular function|rectangular mask function]] can be defined for some bound (''B)'' over time (''t'') as

:: <math>\int_{-\infty}^\infty \int_{-\infty}^\infty |X(t, f)|^2\,df\,dt = 2B \int_{-\infty}^\infty |x(\tau)|^2\,d\tau</math>

Inverse form

The [[jump discontinuity]] of the edges of the rectangular mask results in [[Gibbs phenomenon|Gibbs ringing artifacts]] in the frequency domain
