Given any class ''C'' of similar algebras, '''Jónsson's lemma''' (due to [[Bjarni Jónsson]]) states that if the variety HSP(''C'') generated by ''C'' is [[congruence-distributive variety|congruence-distributive]], its subdirect irreducibles are in HSP<sub>U</sub>(''C''), that is, they are quotients of subalgebras of [[ultraproduct]]s of members of ''C''.

subdirectly irreducible algebras of a congruence-distributive variety generated by a finite set of finite algebras are no larger than the generating algebras
