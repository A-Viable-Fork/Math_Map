In [[universal algebra]], a '''subalgebra''' of an [[structure (mathematical logic)|algebra]] ''A'' is a [[subset]] ''S'' of ''A''

then the only thing that needs to be checked is that ''S'' is [[closure (mathematics)|''closed'']] under the operations.

that also has the structure of an algebra of the same type when the algebraic operations are restricted to ''S''.
