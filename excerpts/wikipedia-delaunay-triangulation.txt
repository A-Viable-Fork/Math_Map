a '''Delaunay triangulation''' or '''Delone triangulation''' of a set of points in the plane subdivides their [[convex hull]]

into triangles whose [[Circumcircle#Triangles|circumcircle]]s do not contain any of the points

This maximizes the size of the smallest angle in any of the triangles, and tends to avoid [[sliver triangle]]s.

corresponds to the [[dual graph]] of the [[Voronoi diagram]] for {{math|'''P'''}}.
