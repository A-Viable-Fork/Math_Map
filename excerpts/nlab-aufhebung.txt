iff it is a minimal level which resolves the opposites of level $i$

The Aufhebung of a level is the smallest level that resolves its opposites or contradictions.

The condition $\bigcirc_j \Box_i=\Box_i$ amounts to saying that every $i$-skeleton is a $j$-sheaf
