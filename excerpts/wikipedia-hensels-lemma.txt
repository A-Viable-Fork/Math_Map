stating that if a [[univariate polynomial]] has a [[simple root]] modulo a [[prime number]] {{math|''p''}}, then this root can be ''lifted'' to a unique root modulo any higher power of {{math|''p''}}.

More generally, if a polynomial factors modulo {{math|''p''}} into two [[coprime polynomials]], this factorization can be lifted to a factorization modulo any higher power of {{math|''p''}}

The proof of Hensel's lemma is [[constructive proof|constructive]], and leads to an efficient algorithm for '''Hensel lifting'''
