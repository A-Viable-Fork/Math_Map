An __exponential object__ $X^Y$ is an [[internal hom]] $[Y,X]$ in a [[cartesian closed category]].

an exponential object $X^Y$ is a [[representing object]] for the functor $\hom(- \times Y, X)$.

Let $X$ and $Y$ be objects of a [[category]] $C$ such that all binary [[products]] with $Y$ exist.

an __exponential object__ is an object $X^Y$ equipped with an __[[evaluation map]]__
