Clifford algebras are closely related to [[exterior algebra]]s.

if {{math|1=''Q'' = 0}} then the Clifford algebra {{math|Cl(''V'', ''Q'')}} is just the exterior algebra {{math|⋀''V''}}.

Cl(''V'', ''Q'')

<math display="block">\dim \operatorname{Cl}(V, Q) = \sum_{k=0}^n \binom{n}{k} = 2^n.

The Clifford algebra is a [[filtered algebra]]; the [[associated graded algebra]] is the exterior algebra.
