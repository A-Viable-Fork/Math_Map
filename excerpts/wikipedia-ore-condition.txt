the '''Ore condition''' is a condition introduced by [[Øystein Ore]], in connection with the question of extending beyond [[commutative ring]]s the construction of a [[field of fractions]]

such that every element of ''D'' is of the form ''rs''<sup>−1</sup> for ''r'' in ''R'' and ''s'' nonzero in ''R''.

or more generally [[localization of a ring]].

Such a division ring ''D'' is called a '''ring of right fractions''' of ''R''

is a right Ore domain if and only if ''D'' is a [[flat module|flat]] left ''R''-module
