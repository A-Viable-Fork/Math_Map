It comes in two forms: a [[long exact sequence]], built from the [[homotopy fiber|mapping fibre]] (a [[fibration]])

:<math>\cdots \to \Omega^2(Mf) \to \Omega^2 X \to \Omega^2 Y \to \Omega(Mf) \to \Omega X \to \Omega Y \to Mf\to X \to Y</math>

Let <math>f\colon (X,x_0)\to(Y,y_0)</math> be a [[continuous map]] between [[pointed space]]s

The construction can then be iterated to obtain the exact Puppe sequence

and a long coexact sequence, built from the [[mapping cone (topology)|mapping cone]] (which is a [[cofibration]])

:<math>A\to B\to C(f) \to \Sigma A \to \Sigma B \to \Sigma C(f) \to \Sigma^2 A \to \Sigma^2 B \to \Sigma^2 C(f) \to \Sigma^3 A \to \Sigma^3 B \to \Sigma^3 C(f) \to \cdots </math>

Let <math>f\colon A \to B</math> be a [[continuous map]] between [[CW complex]]es

one can also consider unreduced and reduced Puppe sequences (at least if dealing with [[pointed space]]s, when it's possible to form reduced suspension).
