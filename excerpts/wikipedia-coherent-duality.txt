'''coherent duality''' is any of a number of generalisations of [[Serre duality]], applying to [[coherent sheaves]]

Grothendieck reformulated general coherent duality as the existence of a [[right adjoint]] functor <math>f^{!}</math>, called ''twisted'' or ''[[exceptional inverse image functor]]'', to a higher [[direct image with compact support]] functor <math>Rf_{!}</math>.

While Serre duality uses a [[line bundle]] or [[invertible sheaf]] as a '''[[dualizing sheaf]]''', the general theory (it turns out) cannot be quite so simple.

The classical statement of Grothendieck duality for a projective or proper morphism <math>f : X \rightarrow Y</math> of noetherian schemes of finite dimension
