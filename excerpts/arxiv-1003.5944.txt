Naively, one might hope that n-skeletal implies (n+1)-coskeletal, and for reﬂexive globular sets this is indeed the case

If a simplicial set is n-skeletal with n > 1, it is (2n− 1)-coskeletal.

The Aufhebung relation for the topos of cubical sets is 2n.
