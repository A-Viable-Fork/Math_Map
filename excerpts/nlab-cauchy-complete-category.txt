The basic idea is that the Cauchy [[completion]] of a [[category]] is the closure of a category under what are called &quot;[[absolute limit]]s&quot;

Equivalently, the Cauchy completion is the closure with respect to [[absolute colimit]]s.

the Cauchy completion of $\mathcal{V}$-enriched category is its completion under _finite direct sums and idempotent splitting_.

The Cauchy completion is the usual completion under _Cauchy nets_ or _Cauchy filters_.

All of these concepts generalize straightforwardly to the context of general $V$-[[enriched category theory|enriched categories]]

It contains $C$ as a [[full subcategory]] because the [[Yoneda embedding]] is a [[full and faithful functor]].

and we say that $C$ and $\bar{C}$ are **Morita equivalent**.

$$Set^{C^{op}} \simeq Set^{\bar{C}^{op}}$$

the inclusion $C \hookrightarrow \overline{C}$ is an [[equivalence of categories]] precisely if already every [[idempotent]] in $C$ splits;
