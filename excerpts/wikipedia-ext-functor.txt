The name comes from the fact that the first Ext group Ext<sup>1</sup> classifies [[group extension|extensions]] of one [[module (mathematics)|module]] by another.

There is a one-to-one correspondence between [[equivalence class]]es of extensions of <math>A</math> by <math>B</math> and elements of <math>\operatorname{Ext}_R^1(A,B)</math>.

classifies [[group extension|extensions]] of one [[module (mathematics)|module]] by another.

the '''Ext functors''' are the [[derived functor]]s of the [[Hom functor]].

For each integer <math>i</math>, <math>\text{Ext}_R^i(A,B)</math> is the [[chain complex|cohomology]] of this complex at position <math>i</math>.

is the set of equivalence classes of extensions of ''A'' by ''B'', forming an abelian group under the Baer sum.

Let <math>R</math> be a ring and let <math>R\text{-Mod}</math> be the [[category (mathematics)|category]] of modules over <math>R</math>.
