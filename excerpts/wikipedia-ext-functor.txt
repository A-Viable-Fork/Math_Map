The name comes from the fact that the first Ext group Ext<sup>1</sup> classifies [[group extension|extensions]] of one [[module (mathematics)|module]] by another.

There is a one-to-one correspondence between [[equivalence class]]es of extensions of <math>A</math> by <math>B</math> and elements of <math>\operatorname{Ext}_R^1(A,B)</math>.
