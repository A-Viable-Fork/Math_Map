A _biproduct_ in a [[category]] $\mathcal{C}$ is an operation that is both a [[product]] and a [[coproduct]], in a compatible way.

This object is often denoted $c_1 \oplus c_2$, alluding to the [[direct sum]] (which is often an example).

Morphisms between finite biproducts are encoded in a [[matrix calculus]].
