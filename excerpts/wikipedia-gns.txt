the '''Gelfand–Naimark–Segal construction''' establishes a correspondence between cyclic <math>*</math>-representations of <math>A</math>

Given a state <math>\rho</math> of <math>A</math>, there is a <math>*</math>-representation <math>\pi</math> of <math>A</math> acting on a Hilbert space <math>H</math> with distinguished unit cyclic vector <math>\xi</math>
