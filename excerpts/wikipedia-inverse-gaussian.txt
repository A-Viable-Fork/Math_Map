the inverse Gaussian describes the distribution of the time a Brownian motion with positive drift takes to reach a fixed positive level.

T_\alpha = \inf\{ t > 0 \mid X_t=\alpha \} \sim \operatorname{IG} \left(\frac\alpha\nu, \left(\frac \alpha \sigma \right)^2 \right)

where <math>\mu > 0</math> is the mean
