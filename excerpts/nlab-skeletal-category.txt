A __skeleton__ of a category $C$ is defined to be a skeletal [[subcategory]] of $C$

is called __skeletal__ if any two [[objects]] that are [[isomorphism|isomorphic]] are actually already [[equality|equal]]

whose [[inclusion functor]] exhibits it as [[equivalence of categories|equivalent]] to $C$.

If the [[axiom of choice]] holds, then every category $C$ has a skeleton (in the strongest sense).
