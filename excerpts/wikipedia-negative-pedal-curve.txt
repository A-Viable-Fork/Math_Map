For a given curve in the plane and a given fixed point ''P'', called the '''pedal point''', the '''negative pedal curve''' is the [[envelope (mathematics)|envelope]] of the lines ''XP'' for which ''X'' lies on the given curve.

The negative pedal curve of a [[pedal curve]] with the same pedal point is the original curve.
