In [[graph theory]], the Y-Δ transform means replacing a Y [[Glossary of graph theory#Subgraphs|subgraph]] of a graph with the equivalent Δ subgraph.

The transform preserves the number of edges in a graph, but not the number of vertices or the number of [[cycle (graph theory)|cycle]]s.
