the '''ham sandwich theorem''' states that given {{mvar|n}} [[measurable]] "objects" in {{mvar|n}}-[[dimension]]al [[Euclidean space]]

to divide each one of them in half (with respect to their [[Measure (mathematics)|measure]], e.g. volume) with a single {{math|(''n'' − 1)}}-dimensional [[Hyperplane#Affine hyperplanes|affine hyperplane]].
