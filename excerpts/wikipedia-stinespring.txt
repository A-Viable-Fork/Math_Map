named after [[W. Forrest Stinespring]]

every completely positive map <math>\Phi</math> can be "[[Lifting property|lifted]]" up to a map of the form <math>V^* (\cdot) V</math>.

In particular <math>V^\ast V = \Phi(1)</math> holds so that <math>V</math> is an isometry if and only if <math>\Phi(1)=1</math>.

represents any [[completely positive map]] on a [[C*-algebra]] ''A''

Thus minimal Stinespring representations are unique [[up to]] a unitary transformation.
