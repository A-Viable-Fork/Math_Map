For a polynomial with coefficients in a [[field (mathematics)|field]], the discriminant is zero [[if and only if]] the polynomial has a [[multiple root]] in an [[algebraically closed extension]].

:<math>\operatorname{Disc}_x(A) = \frac{(-1)^{n(n-1)/2}}{a_n} \operatorname{Res}_x(A,A')</math>
