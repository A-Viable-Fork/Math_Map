'''Birkhoff's representation theorem''' for [[distributive lattice]]s states that the elements of any finite distributive lattice can be represented as [[finite set]]s

the elements of any finite distributive lattice may be associated with lower sets of a partial order in the same way.

In a lattice, an element <math>x</math> is called ''join-irreducible'' if <math>x</math> is not the join of a finite set of other elements.

the elements of any finite distributive lattice can be represented as [[finite set]]s

in such a way that the lattice operations correspond to [[Union (set theory)|unions]] and [[Intersection (set theory)|intersections]] of sets.
