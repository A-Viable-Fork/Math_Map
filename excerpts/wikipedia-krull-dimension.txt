the '''Krull dimension''' of a [[commutative ring]] ''R'', named after [[Wolfgang Krull]], is the [[supremum]] of the lengths of all [[chain (order theory)|chain]]s of [[prime ideals]].

We define the '''Krull dimension''' of <math>R</math> to be the supremum of the lengths of all chains of prime ideals in <math>R</math>.

The Krull dimension need not be finite even for a [[Noetherian ring]].
