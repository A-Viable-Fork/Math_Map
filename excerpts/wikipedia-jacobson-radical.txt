It is also true that J(''R'') equals the intersection of all maximal left ideals within the ring.

The Jacobson radical plays a prominent role in many ring- and module-theoretic results, such as [[Nakayama's lemma]].

For a general ring with unity <math>R</math>

the Jacobson radical <math>J(R)</math> is defined as the ideal of all elements <math>r\in R</math> such that <math>rM=0</math> whenever <math>M</math> is a [[simple module|simple]] <math>R</math>-module.
