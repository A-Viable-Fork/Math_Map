The notion of '''institution''' was created by [[Joseph Goguen]] and [[Rod Burstall]] in the late 1970s

it is assumed that signature morphisms lead to translations of sentences and models in a way that satisfaction is preserved.

'''signature morphisms''' allow to extend signatures, change notation, and so on.

models are translated (or better: reduced) '''against''' signature morphisms.

satisfaction condition expresses that truth is invariant under change of notation
