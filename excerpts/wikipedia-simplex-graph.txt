the '''simplex graph''' {{math|κ(''G'')}} of an [[undirected graph]] {{mvar|G}} is itself a graph, with one [[Vertex (graph theory)|node]] for each [[clique (graph theory)|clique]]

The empty set is included as one of the cliques of {{mvar|G}} that are used to form the clique graph, as is every set of one vertex and every set of two adjacent vertices.

The simplex graph is the [[median graph]] corresponding to this median algebra structure.
