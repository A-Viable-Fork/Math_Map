An '''absolute neighborhood retract''' ('''ANR''') is a particularly [[well-behaved]] type of topological space.

Every ANR has the [[homotopy type]] of a very simple topological space, a [[CW complex]].

A space <math display="inline">X</math> is an '''absolute neighborhood retract''' for the class <math>\mathcal{C}</math>, written <math display="inline">\operatorname{ANR} \left(\mathcal{C}\right),</math> if <math display="inline">X</math> is in <math>\mathcal{C}</math> and whenever <math display="inline">X</math> is a closed subset of a space <math display="inline">Y</math> in <math>\mathcal{C}</math>, <math display="inline">X</math> is a neighborhood retract of <math display="inline">Y</math>.
