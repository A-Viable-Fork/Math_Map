Ordinal addition is, in general, not [[commutative]].

Multiplication of ordinals is not in general commutative.

Ordinal addition on the [[natural numbers]] is the same as standard addition.

The first transfinite ordinal is {{mvar|ω}}, the set of all natural numbers, followed by {{math|''ω'' + 1}}, {{math|''ω'' + 2}}, etc.
