Informally, a free object over a [[Set (mathematics)|set]] ''A'' can be thought of as being a "generic" [[algebraic structure]] over ''A''

the only equations that hold between elements of the free object are those that follow from the defining axioms of the algebraic structure.

{{Short description|Left adjoint to a forgetful functor to sets}}

The concept is a part of [[universal algebra]], in the sense that it relates to all types of algebraic structure (with [[finitary]] operations).
