the '''Bruhat decomposition'''

*<math>B</math> is a [[Borel subgroup]] of <math>G</math>

:<math>G=BWB =\bigsqcup_{w\in W}BwB</math>

as a disjoint union of [[double coset]]s of <math>B</math> parameterized by the elements of the Weyl group <math>W</math>.

The cells in the Bruhat decomposition correspond to the [[Schubert cell]] decomposition of flag varieties. The dimension of the cells corresponds to the [[length function|length]] of the word <math>w</math> in the Weyl group.

*<math>G</math> is a [[connected space|connected]], [[reductive group|reductive]] [[algebraic group]] over an [[algebraically closed field]].
