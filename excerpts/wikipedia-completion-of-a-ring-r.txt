The (''I''-adic) completion is the [[inverse limit]] of the [[factor ring]]s,

: <math> \widehat{R}_I=\varprojlim (R/I^n) </math>

* The completion of a [[Noetherian ring]] <math>R</math> is a [[flat module]] over <math>R</math>.

The kernel of the canonical map {{pi}} from the ring to its completion is the intersection of the powers of ''I''.
