a '''dual curve''' of a given [[plane curve]] {{mvar|C}} is a curve in the [[Duality (projective geometry)|dual projective plane]] consisting of the set of lines [[tangent]] to {{mvar|C}}.

The [[Plücker formula]]s give the degree of the dual in terms of ''d'' and the number and types of singular points of {{mvar|X}}.
