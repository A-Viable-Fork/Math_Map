A _sifted colimit_ is a [[colimit]] of a [[diagram]] $D \to C$ where $D$ is a [[sifted category]]

In fact, sifted colimits can &quot;almost&quot; be characterized as combinations of [[filtered colimits]] and reflexive coequalizers.

Such colimits [[limits commuting with colimits|commute]] with finite [[products]] in [[Set]], by definition.
