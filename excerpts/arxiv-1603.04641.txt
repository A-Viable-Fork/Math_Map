An open game G : (X, S) →
(Y, R) is deﬁned to be a 4-tuple G = (Σ G, PG , CG, BG), where

• BG : X × (Y → R) → Rel(ΣG) is called the best response

• (σ, σ′) ∈ BD(x, k) iff σ′(x) ∈ arg max k

We deﬁne an open game G1 ⊗ G2 : (X1 ×
X2, S1 × S2) → (Y1 × Y2, R1 × R2) as follows:

k1(y1) = π1(k(y1, PG2 (σ2, x2)))
k2(y2) = π2(k(PG1 (σ1, x1), y2))

A selection equilibrium is a pure strategy proﬁle σ with (σ, σ) ∈ B.

Possibly the most important part of the deﬁnition is the best
response relation, which is deﬁned relative to an arbitrary context

k′(y) = CH(τ, y, k(PH(τ, y)))
