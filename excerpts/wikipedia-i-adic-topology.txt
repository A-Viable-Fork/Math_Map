Let {{mvar|R}} be a commutative ring and {{mvar|M}} an {{mvar|R}}-module. Then each [[ideal (ring theory)|ideal]] {{math|𝔞}} of {{mvar|R}} determines a topology on {{mvar|M}} called the {{math|𝔞}}-adic topology

The family <math display=block>\{x+\mathfrak{a}^nM:x\in M,n\in\mathbb{Z}^+\}</math> is a [[basis (topology)|basis]] for this topology.

With respect to the topology, the module operations of addition and scalar multiplication are [[Continuity (topology)|continuous]], so that {{mvar|M}} becomes a [[topological module]].
