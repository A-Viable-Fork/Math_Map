An initial object, if it exists, is unique up to unique [[isomorphism]], so that we may speak of [[the]] initial object.

By definition, an initial object is equipped with a universal [[cocone]] under the unique functor $\emptyset\to C$ from the [[empty category]].

if $I$ is initial, the unique morphisms $!: I \to x$ form a cone
