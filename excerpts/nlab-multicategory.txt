Another term for _multicategory_ is (nonsymmetric/planar)_coloured [[operad]]_.

* A source map $s: C_1 \to (C_0)*$ to the collection of finite, possibly empty [[list]]s of objects

We write $f: c_1, \ldots, c_n \to c$ to indicate the source and target of a multimorphism $f$.
