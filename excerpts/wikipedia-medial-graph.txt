Given a connected [[plane graph]] ''G'', its medial graph ''M(G)'' has

* a vertex for each edge of ''G'' and

* an edge between two vertices for each face of ''G'' in which their corresponding edges occur consecutively.

* For any connected plane graph ''G'', the medial graph of ''G'' and the medial graph of the [[dual graph]] of ''G'' are isomorphic.

For a plane graph ''G'', twice the evaluation of the [[Tutte polynomial]] at the point (3,3) equals the sum over weighted [[Eulerian orientation]]s in the medial graph of ''G''

* Every 4-regular plane graph is the medial graph of some plane graph.
