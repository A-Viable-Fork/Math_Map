the '''evolute''' of a [[curve]] is the [[locus (mathematics)|locus]] of all its [[Center of curvature|centers of curvature]].

is the parametric representation of a [[regular curve]] in the plane with its curvature nowhere 0

Evolutes are closely connected to [[involute]]s: A curve is the evolute of any of its involutes.

*For any arc of the evolute that does not include a cusp, the length of the arc equals the difference between the radii of curvature at its endpoints.
