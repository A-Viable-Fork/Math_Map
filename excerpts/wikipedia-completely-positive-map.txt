and '''completely positive''' if <math>\phi</math> is k-positive for all k.

We then say <math>\phi</math> is '''k-positive''' if <math>\textrm{id}_{\mathbb{C}^{k\times k}} \otimes \phi</math> is a positive map

Let <math>A</math> and <math>B</math> be [[C*-algebra]]s. A linear map <math>\phi: A\to B</math>

is a map between [[C*-algebra]]s that sends positive elements to positive elements.
