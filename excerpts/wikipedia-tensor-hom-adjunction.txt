the '''tensor-hom adjunction''' is the statement that the [[Tensor product of modules|tensor product]]

The order of terms in the phrase "tensor-hom adjunction" reflects their relationship: tensor is the left adjoint, while hom is the right adjoint.

Fix an <math>(R,S)</math>-bimodule <math>X</math>

:<math>\operatorname{Hom}(Y \otimes X, Z) \cong \operatorname{Hom}(Y,\operatorname{Hom}(X,Z)).</math>
