the '''tensor product''' of two [[field (mathematics)|fields]]  is their [[tensor product of algebras|tensor product]] as [[algebra over a field|algebras]] over a common [[subfield (mathematics)|subfield]].

The tensor product of two fields is sometimes a field, and often a [[product ring|direct product]] of fields; in some cases, it can contain non-zero [[nilpotent element]]s.
