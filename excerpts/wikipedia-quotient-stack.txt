Let ''G'' be an affine smooth [[group scheme]] over a scheme ''S'' and ''X'' an ''S''-scheme on which ''G'' [[group-scheme action|acts]]. Let the quotient stack <math>[X/G]</math> be the [[fibered category|category over]] the category of ''S''-schemes

*an object over ''T'' is a [[torsor (algebraic geometry)|principal ''G''-bundle]] <math>P\to T</math> together with equivariant map <math>P\to X</math>;

the space "X/G" is usually coarser.

In general, <math>[X/G]</math> is an [[Artin stack]] (also called algebraic stack). If the stabilizers of the [[geometric point]]s are finite and reduced, then it is a [[Deligne–Mumford stack]].
