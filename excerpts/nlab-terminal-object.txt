A **terminal object** in a [[category]] $C$ is an [[object]] $1$ of $C$ satisfying the following [[universal property]]:

for every object $x$ of $C$, there exists a unique [[morphism]] $!:x\to 1$.

A **terminal object** in a [[category]] $C$ is an [[object]] $1$ of $C$

The terminal object of any category, if it exists, is unique up to unique [[isomorphism]].
