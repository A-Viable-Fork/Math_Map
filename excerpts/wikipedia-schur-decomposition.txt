Given square matrices ''A'' and ''B'', the '''generalized Schur decomposition''' factorizes both matrices as <math>A = QSZ^*</math> and <math>B = QTZ^*</math>, where ''Q'' and ''Z'' are [[unitary matrix|unitary]], and ''S'' and ''T'' are [[upper triangular]]. The generalized Schur decomposition is also sometimes called the '''QZ decomposition'''.

can be calculated as the ratio of the diagonal elements of ''S'' to those of ''T''.
