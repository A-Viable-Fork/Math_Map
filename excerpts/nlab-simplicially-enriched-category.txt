* requiring the hom-simplicial sets to be [[Kan complex]]es makes simplicially enriched categories a model for [[(∞,1)-categories]];

To every [[category with weak equivalences]] $(C,W)$ is associated its [[simplicial localization]] $L_W C$

which is an $sSet$-category

with the property that its [[homotopy category of an (∞,1)-category]] coincides with the [[homotopy category]] $Ho_W(C)$.
