an '''Adams operation''', denoted ψ<sup>''k''</sup> for natural numbers ''k'',  is a [[cohomology operation]] in [[topological K-theory]]

In (algebraic or topological) K-theory, Adams operations <math>\psi^k</math> are [[ring homomorphism]]s such that for <math>k\in\N</math>,

* <math>\psi^k(l)=l^k</math> if <math>l</math> is the class of a [[line bundle]],

* <math>\psi^k</math> are [[functorial]].
