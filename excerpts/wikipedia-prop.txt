The notion was introduced by [[Frank Adams]] and [[Saunders Mac Lane]]

whose objects are the natural numbers ''n'' identified with the finite sets <math>\{0,1,\ldots,n-1\}</math> and whose tensor product is given on objects by the addition on numbers.

a '''PROP''' is a [[symmetric monoidal category|symmetric]] strict [[monoidal category]]
