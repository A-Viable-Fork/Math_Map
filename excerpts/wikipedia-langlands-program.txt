Roughly speaking, this conjecture gives a correspondence between automorphic representations of a reductive group and homomorphisms from a [[Langlands group]] to an [[Langlands dual group|''L''-group]].

this conjecture relates their automorphic representations in a way that is compatible with their ''L''-functions.
