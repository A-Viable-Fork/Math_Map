the '''bipartite double cover''' of an [[undirected graph]] {{mvar|G}} is a [[bipartite graph|bipartite]], [[covering graph]] of {{mvar|G}}, with twice as many [[Vertex (graph theory)|vertices]] as {{mvar|G}}.

It can be constructed as the [[tensor product of graphs]], {{math|''G'' × ''K''{{sub|2}}}}.
