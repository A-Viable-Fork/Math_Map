the '''two-sided Laplace transform''' or '''bilateral Laplace transform''' is an [[integral transform]] equivalent to [[probability]]'s [[moment-generating function]].

<math>\mathcal{B}\{f\}(s) = F(s) = \int_{-\infty}^\infty e^{-st} f(t)\, dt.</math>

The [[Fourier transform]] can be defined in terms of the two-sided Laplace transform {{tmath|F}} of a function {{tmath|f}}:

| <math> \alpha < \Re(s) < \beta </math>

| <math> (f * g)(t) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(\tau)\,g(t - \tau)\,d\tau </math>

then {{tmath|1= f=g }} [[almost everywhere]].

Bilateral transforms do not respect [[causality]].
