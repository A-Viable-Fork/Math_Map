The natural [[tensor product]] operation on [[finite abelian category|finite abelian categories]] is known as the _Deligne tensor product_ or _Deligne box product_

right [[exact functors]] of the form $A \boxtimes B \to C$ are equivalent to functors $A \times B \to C$ that are right exact in each argument separately.

This tensor product exists for [[finite abelian categories]] but not generally on all [[abelian categories]].
