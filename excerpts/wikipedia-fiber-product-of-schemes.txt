the '''fiber product of schemes''' is a fundamental construction.

That is, for any morphisms of schemes ''X'' → ''Y'' and ''Z'' → ''Y'', there is a scheme ''X'' ×<sub>''Y''</sub> ''Z'' with morphisms to ''X'' and ''Z''

and which is [[universal property|universal]] with that property.
