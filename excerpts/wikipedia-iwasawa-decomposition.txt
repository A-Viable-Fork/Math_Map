the '''Iwasawa decomposition''' (aka '''KAN''' from its expression) of a [[semisimple Lie group]]

:<math>G=KAN</math>

*''G'' is a connected semisimple real [[Lie group]].

is a [[nilpotent Lie algebra]] given as the  sum of the root spaces of Σ<sup>+</sup>

is a maximal abelian subalgebra of <math> \mathfrak{p}_0 </math>

where ''K'' becomes a (disconnected) [[maximal compact subgroup]]

meaning there is an analytic [[diffeomorphism]] (but not a group homomorphism) from the [[manifold]] <math> K \times A \times N </math> to the Lie group <math> G </math>

(but not a group homomorphism)

*<math> \mathfrak{a}_0 </math> is a maximal abelian subalgebra of <math> \mathfrak{p}_0 </math>

*<math> \mathfrak{n}_0 </math> is a [[nilpotent Lie algebra]]

meaning there is an analytic [[diffeomorphism]] (but not a group homomorphism) from the [[manifold]] <math> K \times A \times N </math> to the Lie group <math> G </math>, sending <math> (k,a,n) \mapsto kan </math>.

*''K'', ''A'', ''N'', are the Lie subgroups of ''G'' generated by <math> \mathfrak{k}_0, \mathfrak{a}_0 </math> and <math> \mathfrak{n}_0 </math>.
