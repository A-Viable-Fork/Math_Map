a '''dagger category''' (also called '''involutive category''' or '''category with involution'''

equipped with an [[Involution (mathematics)|involutive]] [[Contravariant functor|contravariant]] [[endofunctor]] <math>\dagger</math> which is the identity on [[Object (category theory)|objects]].

the map <math>f^\dagger:B \rightarrow A</math> is just its [[Hermitian adjoint|adjoint]] in the usual sense.

* for all [[morphism]]s <math>f: A \to B</math>, there exists its [[Hermitian adjoint|adjoint]] <math>f^\dagger: B \to A</math>

* for all morphisms <math>f</math>, <math>(f^\dagger)^\dagger = f</math>

<math>(g \circ f)^\dagger = f^\dagger \circ g^\dagger: C \to A</math>

there exists its [[Hermitian adjoint|adjoint]] <math>f^\dagger: B \to A</math>
