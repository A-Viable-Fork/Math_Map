This is also known as the ''Krasner–Kaloujnine embedding theorem''.

If <math>G</math> is an [[Group extension|extension]] of <math>A</math> by <math>H</math>, then there exists a subgroup of the unrestricted wreath product <math>A\wr H</math> which is isomorphic to <math>G</math>.
