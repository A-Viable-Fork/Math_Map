'''Frobenius reciprocity''' is a theorem expressing a [[Duality (mathematics)|duality]] between the process of [[Restricted representation|restricting]] and [[Induced representation|inducing]].

These functors form an [[Adjoint functors|adjoint pair]] <math>\operatorname{Ind}_H^G\dashv\operatorname{Res}_H^G</math>.

In the case of finite groups, they are actually both left- and right-adjoint to one another.
