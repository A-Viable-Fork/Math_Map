the concept of a '''semidirect product''' is a generalization of a [[direct product of groups|direct product]].

of groups (which is also known as a [[Group_extension#Classifying_split_extensions | split extension]] of <math>H</math> by <math>N</math>).

In other words, there is a split [[exact sequence]] <math display="block">1 \to N \to G \to H \to 1</math>

an ''inner'' semidirect product is a particular way in which a [[Group (mathematics)|group]] can be made up of two [[subgroup]]s, one of which is a [[normal subgroup]].
