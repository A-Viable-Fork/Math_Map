An _end_ is a special kind of [[limit]] over a [[functor]] of the form $F : C^{op} \times C \to D$

A classical example of an _end_ is the $V$-object of [[natural transformations]] between $V$-[[enriched functors]]

then the _end_ of the functor picks out the universal [[subobject]] on which the left and right actions coincide.

Dually, the _coend_ of $F$ is the universal quotient of $\coprod_{c \in C} F(c,c)$ that forces the two actions of $F$ on that object to be equal.

Perhaps the most common way in which ends and coends arise is through homs and tensor products of (generalized) modules
