The fundamental interest in Dynkin diagrams is that they classify [[semisimple Lie algebra]]s over [[algebraically closed field]]s.

* <math>A_n</math>: <math>\mathfrak {sl}_{n+1}</math>, the [[special linear Lie algebra]].

One classifies such Lie algebras via their [[root system]], which can be represented by a Dynkin diagram.
