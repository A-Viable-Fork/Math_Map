the '''Fox derivative''' is an [[algebra]]ic construction in the theory of [[free group]]s which bears many similarities to the conventional [[derivative]] of [[calculus]].

If <math>G</math> is a free group with identity element <math>e</math> and [[generating set of a group|generators]] <math>g_i</math>, then the Fox derivative with respect to <math>g_i</math> is a function from <math>G</math> into the [[integral group ring]] <math>\Z G</math>

* <math>\frac{\partial}{\partial g_i}(uv) = \frac{\partial}{\partial g_i}(u) + u\frac{\partial}{\partial g_i}(v)</math> for any elements <math>u</math> and <math>v</math> of <math>G</math>.

The Fox derivative has applications in [[group cohomology]], [[knot theory]], and [[covering space]] theory, among other areas of mathematics.
