formal power series can be viewed as a generalization of [[polynomial]]s where the number of terms is allowed to be infinite

(It can be seen as the [[I-adic topology#Completion|{{math|(''x'')}}-adic completion]] of the [[polynomial ring]] <math>R[x],</math>

* if <math>R</math> is [[noetherian ring|Noetherian]], then so is <math>R[[X]]</math> (a version of the [[Hilbert basis theorem]]),

the [[field of fractions]] of the [[integral domain]] <math>K[[X]]</math>.

the ring <math>K[[X_1, \ldots, X_r]]</math> is often used as the "standard, most general" complete local ring over ''K'' in algebra.

and differ from usual [[power series]] by the absence of convergence requirements
