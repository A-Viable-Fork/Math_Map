Formally, a graph [[rewriting]] system usually consists of a set of graph rewrite rules of the form <math>L \rightarrow R</math>, with <math>L</math> being called pattern graph (or left-hand side) and <math>R</math> being called replacement graph

A graph rewrite rule is applied to the host graph by searching for an occurrence of the pattern graph ([[pattern matching]], thus solving the [[subgraph isomorphism problem]]) and by replacing the found occurrence by an instance of the replacement
