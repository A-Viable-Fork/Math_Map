an '''ambient isotopy''', also called an ''h-isotopy'', is a kind of [[continuous map|continuous]] distortion of an [[ambient space]], for example a [[manifold]], taking a [[submanifold]] to another submanifold.

For example in [[knot theory]], one considers two [[knot (mathematics)|knot]]s the same if one can distort one knot into the other without breaking it. Such a distortion is an example of an ambient isotopy.

let <math>N</math> and <math>M</math> be manifolds and <math>g</math> and <math>h</math> be [[embedding]]s of <math>N</math> in <math>M</math>.
