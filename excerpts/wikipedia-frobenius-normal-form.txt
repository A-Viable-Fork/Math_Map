the '''Frobenius normal form''' or  '''rational canonical form''' of a [[square matrix|square]] [[matrix (mathematics)|matrix]] ''A'' with entries in a [[field (mathematics)|field]] ''F'' is a [[canonical form]] for matrices obtained by conjugation by [[invertible matrices]] over ''F''.

The last of these factors ''f<sub>k</sub>'' is then the minimal polynomial

For each invariant factor ''f<sub>i</sub>'' one takes its [[companion matrix]] ''C''<sub>''f<sub>i</sub>''</sub>, and the block diagonal matrix formed from these blocks yields the '''rational canonical form''' of ''A''.

The Frobenius normal form does not reflect any form of factorization of the characteristic polynomial, even if it does exist over the ground field ''F''.
