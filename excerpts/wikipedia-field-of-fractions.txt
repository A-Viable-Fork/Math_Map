the '''field of fractions''' of an [[integral domain]]

The field of fractions of an integral domain <math>R</math> is sometimes denoted by <math>\operatorname{Frac}(R)</math>

the smallest [[field (mathematics)|field]] in which it can be [[Embedding|embedded]].

Intuitively, it consists of ratios between integral domain elements.
