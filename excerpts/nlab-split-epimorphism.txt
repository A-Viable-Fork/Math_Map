A **split epimorphism** in a [[category]] $C$ is a [[morphism]] $e\colon A \to B$ in $C$ such that there exists a morphism $s\colon B \to A$

* $B$ is a __[[retract]]__ of $A$,

* $s$ is a __[[section]]__ of $e$,

it is also possible to define a split epimorphism as an __absolute epimorphism__: a morphism such that for every [[functor]] $F$ out of $C$, $F(e)$ is an [[epimorphism]].
