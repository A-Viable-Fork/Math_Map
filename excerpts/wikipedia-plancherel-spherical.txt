The transformation ''U'' is called the '''spherical Fourier transform''' or sometimes just the '''spherical transform'''

consisting of compactly supported ''K''-biinvariant continuous functions on ''G''

due in its final form to [[Harish-Chandra]]

Two different characters λ<sub>1</sub> and λ<sub>2</sub> give the same spherical function if and only if λ<sub>1</sub> = λ<sub>2</sub>·''s'', where ''s'' is in the [[Weyl group]] of ''A''

and μ is called the '''[[Plancherel measure]]'''

Here '''c'''(λ) is '''Harish-Chandra's c-function'''.
