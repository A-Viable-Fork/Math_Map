the '''cone''' '''of a [[topological space]]''' <math>X</math> is intuitively obtained by stretching ''X'' into a [[Cylinder (geometry)|cylinder]] and then collapsing one of its end faces to a point.

Furthermore, every cone is [[contractible]] to the vertex point by the [[homotopy]]

The cone is used in algebraic topology precisely because it [[Embedding|embeds]] a space as a [[subspace (topology)|subspace]] of a contractible space.

where <math>v</math> is a [[point (topology)|point]] (called the vertex of the cone) and <math>p</math> is the [[Projection (mathematics)|projection]] to that point.
