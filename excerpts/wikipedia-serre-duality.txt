'''Serre duality''' is a [[duality (mathematics)|duality]] for the [[coherent sheaf cohomology]] of algebraic varieties

The basic version applies to [[vector bundle]]s on a smooth projective variety

Then Serre duality says: for an [[algebraic vector bundle]] ''E'' on ''X'' and an integer ''i'', there is a natural isomorphism:

:<math>H^i(X,E)\cong H^{n-i}(X,K_X\otimes E^{\ast})^{\ast}</math>

:<math>h^i(X,E)=h^{n-i}(X,K_X\otimes E^{\ast}).</math>
