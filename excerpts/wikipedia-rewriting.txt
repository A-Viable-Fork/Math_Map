A ''rewrite rule'' is a pair of [[Term (logic)|terms]], commonly written as <math>l \rightarrow r</math>

Schematic triangle diagram of application of a rewrite rule <math>l \longrightarrow r</math> at position <math>p</math> in a term, with matching substitution <math>\sigma</math>

to indicate that the left-hand side {{math|''l''}} can be replaced by the right-hand side {{math|''r''}}.
