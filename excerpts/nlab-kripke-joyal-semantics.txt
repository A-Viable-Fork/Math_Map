**Kripke--Joyal semantics** is a higher order generalization of the [[semantics|semantic]] interpretation proposed initially by Beth, Grzegorczyk, and Kripke

It provides a notion of _&#39;local truth&#39;_ or _&#39;validity at a stage&#39;_ in a [[topos]].

for [[intuitionistic logic|intuitionistic predicate logic]

it is sometimes called _forcing_ semantics.
