the '''complete orthogonal decomposition''' is a [[matrix decomposition]].

<math>A = U T V^*</math>, where <math>U</math> and <math>V^*</math> are [[unitary matrix|unitary matrices]] and <math>T</math> is a [[triangular matrix]].

For a matrix <math>A</math> of [[matrix rank|rank]] <math>r</math>, the triangular matrix <math>T</math> can be chosen such that only its top-left <math>r\times r</math> block is nonzero, making the decomposition rank-revealing.

is a special case of the UTV decomposition.  Computing the SVD is slightly more expensive than the UTV decomposition

but has a stronger rank-revealing property.
