The **little $k$-disk operad** or **little $k$-cubes operad**

whose $n$-ary operations are parameterized by rectilinear disjoint embeddings of $n$ $k$-dimensional cubes into another $k$-dimensional cube.

Then $Y$ is homotopy equivalent to a $k$-fold loop space $\Omega^k X$ for some pointed topological space $X$.

Specifically for $\mathcal{X} = Top$, this refines to the classical theorem by [May 1972](#May72).

Let $Y$ be a [[topological space]] equipped with an action of the [[little cubes operad]] $\mathcal{C}_k$ and suppose that $Y$ is grouplike.

An [[algebra over an operad]] over $E_k$ is an [[Ek-algebra]].

Therefore the [[algebra over an operad|algebras over]] the $E_k$ operad are &quot;$k$-fold monoidal&quot; objects.
