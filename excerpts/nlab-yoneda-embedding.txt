The **Yoneda embedding** for $C$ a [[locally small category]] is the [[functor]]

The [[Yoneda lemma]] implies that this functor is [[full and faithful functor|full and faithful]] and hence realizes $C$ as a [[full subcategory]] inside its category of presheaves.

For $C$ a [[locally small category]], every [[object]] $X$ of $C$ induces a [[presheaf]] on $C$: the [[representable presheaf]] $h_X$ represented by $X$.

This assignment extends to a [[functor]] $C \to [C^{op}, Set]$ from $C$ to its [[category of presheaves]].

The Yoneda embedding $y \colon \mathcal{C} \longrightarrow Func(\mathcal{C}^{op}, Set)$ [[preserved limit|preserves]] [[limits]], hence is a [[continuous functor]].

In contrast, the Yoneda embedding does not in general preserve [[colimits]].

Instead, the Yoneda embedding of a [[small category]] $\mathcal{C}$ is its *[[free cocompletion]]*.
