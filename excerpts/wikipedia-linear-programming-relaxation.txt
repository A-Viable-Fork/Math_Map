the '''relaxation''' of a [[Mixed integer linear programming|(mixed) integer linear program]] is the problem that arises by removing the integrality constraint of each variable.

the solution quality of the linear program is at least as good as that of the integer program, because any integer program solution would also be a valid linear program solution.

an important concept is the '''integrality gap''', the maximum ratio between the solution quality of the integer program and of its relaxation.
