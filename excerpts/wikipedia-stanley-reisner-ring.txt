Given an [[abstract simplicial complex]] Δ on the vertex set {''x''<sub>1</sub>,...,''x''<sub>''n''</sub>} and a field '''k'''

: <math> I_\Delta=(x_{i_1}\ldots x_{i_r}: \{i_1,\ldots,i_r\}\notin\Delta), \quad k[\Delta]=k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/I_\Delta. </math>

* The multigraded, or ''fine'', [[Hilbert series]] of '''k'''[Δ] is given by the formula

\sum_{\sigma\in\Delta}\prod_{i\in\sigma}\frac{x_i}{1-x_i}. </math>

then the coefficients (''h''<sub>0</sub>, ..., ''h''<sub>''d''</sub>) of the numerator form the ''h''-vector of the simplicial complex&nbsp;Δ.
