'''eigendecomposition''' (also known as '''eigenvalue decomposition''' or '''EVD''') is a [[matrix factorization|factorization]] of a [[matrix (mathematics)|matrix]] <math>A</math> into a [[canonical form]] given by {{tmath|A {{=}} QDQ^\mathsf{-1} }}, where <math>D</math> is a diagonal matrix containing the [[eigenvalues]] of <math>A</math> on the diagonal

When the matrix being factorized is a [[normal matrix|normal]] or real [[symmetric matrix]], the decomposition is called "spectral decomposition"

Note that only [[diagonalizable matrices]] can be factorized in this way.

The form is useful because it reduces many matrix operations including powers and matrix functions, to operations on the diagonal entries of Λ.
