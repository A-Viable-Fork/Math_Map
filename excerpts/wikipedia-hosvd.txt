The second equality is because the <math>{\bf U}_m</math>'s are unitary matrices. Define now the '''core tensor'''<math display="block">\mathcal{S} := \mathcal{A} \times ({\bf U}_1^H, {\bf U}_2^H, \ldots, {\bf U}_M^H).</math>

The compact M-mode SVD(HOSVD) is a rank-revealing decomposition in the sense that the dimensions of its core tensor correspond with the components of the multilinear rank of the tensor.
