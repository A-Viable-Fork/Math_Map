Lie algebras were introduced to study the concept of [[infinitesimal transformation]]s by [[Sophus Lie]] in the 1870s,

The name ''Lie algebra'' was given by [[Hermann Weyl]] in the 1930s; in older texts, the term ''infinitesimal group'' was used.

The simple Lie algebras of finite dimension over an [[algebraically closed field]] ''F'' of characteristic zero were classified by Killing and Cartan in the 1880s and 1890s, using [[root system]]s.
