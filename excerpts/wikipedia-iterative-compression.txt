'''iterative compression''' is an [[algorithm]]ic technique for the design of [[parameterized complexity|fixed-parameter tractable algorithms]], in which one element (such as a [[vertex (graph theory)|vertex of a graph]]) is added to the problem in each step, and a small solution for the problem prior to the addition is used to help find a small solution to the problem after the step.

The technique was invented by Reed, Smith and Vetta
